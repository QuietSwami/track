Getting started

  1. Install Project Time from Google Workspace Marketplace and authorize its read-only Calendar permission.
  2. Open Google Calendar on desktop and select Project Time from the right-hand sidebar.
  3. In Settings, choose the calendars that represent projects and save.
  4. Select a week or month, then click a project row for its detailed statistics.

Common fixes

  • Missing icon: reload Calendar, expand the side panel, and confirm the add-on is installed for the same Google account.
  • Authorization error: uninstall and reinstall the add-on, then grant its requested Calendar permissions.
  • Missing calendar: confirm the signed-in account can access it, then reopen Settings.
  • Unexpected total: verify the reporting time zone, first weekday, weekend setting, declined/all-day/Free rules, and overlap notice. Click Refresh after editing events.
